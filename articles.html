Continuous and block processing

A one-step run across 78.007 million active cells took 123.6 seconds in continuous mode and 476.7 seconds in blocks. The recorded outputs matched; annual map export was disabled.

Interpret the evidence

Matching output is a software check.

Agreement between two engines shows that they computed the same states for the tested inputs and settings. It does not establish that the simulated changes accurately predict ecological outcomes.
